## 1. Sharper positioning — the triad

The research verdict: each ingredient on its own is contested. **CMO + AI** is
already commoditizing; **"operator not advisor"** is a saturated claim; **web
migration** is sold by separate web agencies (and mostly in the *opposite*
direction — onto builders, not off them). The defensible position is the
**fusion of all three under one senior operator**, because no established
competitor category bundles them.

### Category
**Marketing operating partner** — not a strategy consultant, not an automation
shop, not a web agency. Strategy that ships as working infrastructure.

### One-liner
> Senior marketing leadership that builds the system — strategy, AI
> infrastructure, and a growth-ready website, from one operator.

### The "only" statement (the wedge)
> The only fractional CMO who personally builds your marketing AI system **and**
> moves you off website-builder limits — so strategy lands as systems your team
> actually uses, not decks that die in a drawer.

### Positioning statement (internal)
For **B2B SaaS and service companies** that need senior marketing direction but
not a full-time CMO, **Professor** is the **fractional marketing operating
partner** that **turns strategy into working AI systems and growth-ready web
infrastructure**. Unlike **strategy-only fractional CMOs, automation shops, and
web agencies — who each own only one piece** — Professor **delivers all three as
one accountable operator**, which is why the work keeps compounding after the
engagement ends.

### Three messaging pillars (map 1:1 to the triad)
1. **Direction** — Fractional CMO leadership: positioning, growth priorities,
   team orchestration, executive judgment.
2. **Systems** — A company-level marketing AI system: knowledge, execution,
   decision, and speed layers that raise throughput without lowering quality.
3. **Infrastructure** — Migration off website-builder ceilings into a flexible,
   conversion-focused custom site built for growth and AI-enabled execution.

### Reasons to believe (proof, not adjectives)
- **One operator, one accountability line** — you don't stitch together a CMO,
  an automation vendor, and a web agency.
- **Built for adoption** — directly answers the documented failure mode where
  95% of GenAI pilots deliver zero business impact (cause: alignment, sponsorship,
  data-readiness — not the tech). Senior judgment is what makes AI stick.
- **Systems that compound** — knowledge, workflows, and web infrastructure keep
  creating value after launch.
- **Bilingual EN/UA** — full-fidelity delivery in two markets.

### Anti-positioning (say what you are NOT)
- Not a deck-and-disappear consultant (the "strategy deck graveyard").
- Not an n8n/Zapier automation shop selling tactics as "proprietary AI."
- Not a web agency that migrates you *onto* a builder and leaves.

### Sharpened hero copy (drop-in candidates)
- **H1:** "Build a stronger marketing operating system." *(fixes the current
  garbled EN h1 "System a stronger marketing operating system")*
- **Sub:** "Senior marketing leadership that ships as working systems — sharper
  positioning, a company-level marketing AI system, and a website built for
  growth. One operator, not three vendors."

## 3. Pricing & packaging model

Three productized stages — a low-risk entry that earns the right to the build,
a fixed-scope build, and an ongoing operating retainer. Productizing (fixed
scope, fixed price) is the dominant, scalable model in this space; it also makes
the offer legible to buyers comparing you against single-category vendors.

### The ladder

**① Marketing System Audit** — *paid diagnostic, the entry point*
- 2–3 weeks. Find the real bottleneck (positioning, execution drag, weak web
  infra, or "AI activity" with no impact) and deliver a prioritized roadmap.
- Fully creditable toward a Build or Operate engagement.
- **Western: $3,000–$5,000 fixed.**

**② Build Sprint** — *fixed-scope project, where the system gets made*
- One or more of: marketing AI system architecture, web migration off a builder,
  positioning/messaging reset. Scoped from the Audit.
- **Western: $15,000–$40,000** (single track ~$15–25k; full triad build ~$30–40k).

**③ Operating Partner** — *monthly retainer, ongoing leadership + iteration*
| Tier | Commitment | What it's for | Western /mo |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Advisory** | ~1–2 days/wk | Direction, prioritization, oversight of an existing team | **$6,000–$9,000** |
| **Operator** | ~3 days/wk | Leadership + hands-on system/web iteration | **$10,000–$15,000** |

*Anchors: US fractional CMO $8–22k/mo (most $12–15k for 2–3 days); UK
£4.5–8k/mo; AI build projects $2.5–15k SMB / $25–100k mid-market; Webflow
migration $0.5–10k typical.*

### Two-market strategy

The Western market is mature and pays $8–15k+/mo. Ukraine/EE is an
education-phase market with a much lower ceiling (IT CMO comp ~$4.75–7.9k/mo;
general-sector far lower). **Don't anchor your pricing to the local floor.**

- **Primary engine — sell Western at Western rates.** Like Algocentric (a
  Ukrainian-founded operator selling outbound to NA/UK/Israel), use EN/UA fluency
  for reach and trust, not as a discount signal. Rates above are the default.
- **Ukraine/EE — value-based, not labor-based.** Lead with the Audit (a
  category buyers there are still being educated on), then price Build/Operate
  against the *cost of the alternative they know* (a full local CMO hire / a
  fragmented stack of specialists), typically **~40–60% of the Western figures**.
  Keep the productized structure identical so the offer reads as the same
  premium category, not a cheaper one.

### Why this shape wins
- The **Audit** de-risks the sale and disqualifies bad-fit buyers cheaply.
- The **Build** captures the AI-systems + migration value no single-category
  competitor can quote against.
- The **Retainer** converts the compounding-systems story into recurring revenue
  and maps cleanly to the day-rate norms buyers already benchmark.
